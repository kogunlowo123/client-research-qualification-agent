Northwind Industries Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2025 Results

February 12, 2026

CHICAGO, Feb. 12, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Northwind Industries, Inc. (NYSE: NWND) today reported financial results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

Total revenue was $12.4 billion for fiscal 2025, an increase of 8% year over year. Fourth quarter revenue of $3.3 billion grew 11%, and diluted EPS was $2.14. Non-GAAP operating margin expanded to 17.5%.

"We are modernizing our ERP estate and consolidating analytics onto a data lakehouse built on Databricks and Microsoft Azure," said Maria Chen, Chief Executive Officer. "Generative AI pilots across customer service are moving into production in the first quarter of 2026."

Leadership update

The company also appointed Rahul Mehta as Chief Information Officer, effective March 1, 2026. Northwind employs approximately 42,000 employees across 30 countries.
